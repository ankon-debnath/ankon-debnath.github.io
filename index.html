About Me

I am a mechanical engineering student interested in additive manufacturing, polymer materials, computational mechanics, and nature-inspired structures.

My current research interests include multimaterial 3D printing, polymer interfaces, nature-inspired architectures, and finite element analysis.

Research Interests

Additive Manufacturing

Polymer 3D printing, FDM/FFF, and multimaterial additive manufacturing.

Nature-Inspired Structures

Bio-inspired architectures such as nacre-like structures for improved mechanical performance.

Computational Mechanics

Finite element analysis and numerical modeling using tools such as Abaqus.

Projects

Low-Cost 3D Printer & Recycled PET Filament

Development of a low-cost 3D printing system and investigation of filament generation from recycled PET bottles.

Finite Element Analysis of 3D Printed Structures

Numerical investigation and validation of the mechanical behavior of 3D printed polymer structures using finite element analysis.
